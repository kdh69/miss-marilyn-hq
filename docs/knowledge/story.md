# Story and page plan
Last reviewed: 2026-11-11

What a Miss Marilyn story has to be, and what Book 1's drafts taught. The series bible says what
the books are; this file holds the evidence behind the numbers and the open questions.

## Rules

- 24 pages including the title and copyright pages: 10 story spreads, story text on 11 to 15 pages, 250 to 400 words in all, under 50 words a spread. [proven, checked 2026-11-01] [handoff](../HANDOFF.md)
- One adventure, one lesson, the "Achoo!" gag at least once, and the "Meet the Real Miss Marilyn" page last, with a photo placeholder. [proven, checked 2026-11-11] [story checklist](../../apps/backend/rubrics/story.md)
- Count the words yourself, per page, per spread and in total, before saying you're done. [proven, checked 2026-11-11] [story checklist](../../apps/backend/rubrics/story.md)
- At most 5 lines of body text on a page, about 35 to 45 characters a line. A page that needs more is too long: shorten the text, never the font. [proven, checked 2026-11-11] [style guide](../brand/style-guide.md)
- When the book starts from Clara's own story, keep her events, her characters and her ending. [proven, checked 2026-11-01] [handoff](../HANDOFF.md)
- Write a draft that is easy to rewrite: Clara puts every page into her own words, and only her words print. [proven, checked 2026-11-11] [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md)
- Every page's `art_brief` names who is in the picture, the one moment, the mood and the text zone (top band, bottom band or side column). [proven, checked 2026-11-11] [style guide](../brand/style-guide.md)
- Zoey appears only when the brief or Clara's story includes her. [proven, checked 2026-11-01] [handoff](../HANDOFF.md)

## What our own runs showed

- Story v1 (T-0024) was replaced: Kyle's brief for v2 asked for a warmer telling without the "Hello first!" refrain. A refrain is optional; don't force one. [proven, checked 2026-11-01] [handoff](../HANDOFF.md)
- Story v2 (T-0038) passed QC at about 270 words, text on 15 pages, the longest spread 49 words, "Achoo" three times with Gussie's echo, and a proud ending. [proven, checked 2026-11-01] [handoff](../HANDOFF.md)
- Even so, p14 set at 6 lines against the style guide's 5. Word count alone doesn't catch a page that is too long; check lines too. [proven, checked 2026-11-01] [handoff](../HANDOFF.md)
- A picture can miss a beat the words depend on: the drafts had no tickle face on p16 before p17's ACHOO. When a gag is set up on one page and pays off on the next, say so in both art briefs. [proven, checked 2026-11-01] [handoff](../HANDOFF.md)
