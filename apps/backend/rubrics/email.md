# QC rubric — email

Compare against the brief (audience segment, purpose, product, send window) and the assets referenced.

Blocking:
- Missing subject line, preview text, body or a single clear call to action
- Links missing UTM tags (utm_source=email, utm_medium=email, utm_campaign={product_id}) or pointing to the wrong product
- Hype or unbackable claims; fake urgency ("only 2 left") on digital or print-on-demand items
- Any photo of a child, real customer's name, or testimonial we can't trace to a source
- Addresses children instead of parents
- No unsubscribe line placeholder, or no sender name and mailing address placeholder
- Facts that contradict the product (wrong age range, page count, price, format)
Minor: subject over ~50 characters, body longer than it needs to be, voice not warm parent to parent.
