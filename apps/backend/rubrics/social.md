# QC rubric — social_batch

Compare against the brief (product, week, channels), the content rows in this batch, the assets they
reference and the book's approved marketing plan (plans rows, status approved).
One batch = one week of posts for one product, one content row per post. The owner posts each one by
hand from the panel's Social page, so every row must be ready to paste.

Blocking:
- A content row missing channel, content_type, scheduled_for (YYYY-MM-DD), hook, body, asset_ids or link
- Pace or platforms that don't follow the approved plan's platform rows
- hashtags missing, over the plan's count, or naming another brand, book or character
- notes missing the one thing the owner needs to post it (Pinterest board, sound, sticker, alt text)
- Posts not inside the brief's week, or more than one product in the batch
- Link missing UTM tags or wrong: must carry utm_source={channel}, utm_medium=social, utm_campaign={product_id}
- Any photo or video of a child, any real customer's name, or audio without a licence
- Posts that speak to children instead of parents
- Hype or unbackable claims ("best book ever", "teaches reading", "#1", awards we don't have)
- asset_ids pointing to assets that don't exist, aren't QC-passed, or don't belong to this product
- Post body over the channel's limit, or a hook that doesn't match the asset shown
Minor: voice drifts from warm, playful, parent to parent; too few posts lead with the real cat; repetitive hooks.
