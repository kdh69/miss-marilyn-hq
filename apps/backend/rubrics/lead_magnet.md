# QC rubric — lead_magnet

The lead magnet is a free printable given for joining the email list. Compare against the brief,
the style guide and the printable rubric rules. Preflight already checks page size, DPI and fonts.

Blocking:
- Fails any blocking item in the printable rubric (edges, line weight, activity works, on-model)
- Not clearly worth an email address: under 3 usable pages, or a cut-down page from a paid product
  the brief didn't approve giving away
- No small footer with the brand name and where to find the books (no hard sell on every page)
- Signup-page copy missing, or promises something the file doesn't contain
- Any photo of a child or real customer's name
- Copy addresses children instead of parents, or makes claims we can't back
Minor: weak title for the signup page, ink-heavy pages, page order.
