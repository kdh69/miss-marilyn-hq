# QC rubric — art_cover

Compare against the cover brief, the character sheet, the style guide and the book's interior art.
Resolution and bleed are checked by preflight, not by you.

Blocking:
- Any text, letters, logos or watermark in the image (title and author are added by the Builder)
- Miss Marilyn off-model: markings, colors, proportions or collar differ from the character sheet
- Miss Marilyn not the clear focal point, or not recognisable at thumbnail size (~1 in wide)
- No calm area for the title in the top third and for the author line at the bottom; busy detail where text must sit
- Important content within ~0.375 in of the trim edge, or crossing where the spine fold would be
- Scene doesn't show this book's adventure, or looks like a different art style from the interior
- Anatomy errors a child would notice (extra paws, merged bodies, wrong number of tails)
- Anything frightening, or resembling an existing book cover, character or brand
Minor: palette drift from the style guide, background clutter, weak contrast at thumbnail size.
