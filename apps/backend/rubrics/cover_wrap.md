# QC rubric — cover_wrap

Compare against the passed cover art, the KDP packet (title, subtitle, series, author) and the style guide.
Preflight already checks overall size, bleed, DPI, embedded fonts, the spine width formula and live
text in the spine. Do not re-judge those; judge the rendered wrap.

Blocking:
- Front: title, series badge or author name missing, misspelled, or different from the KDP packet
- Front and back swapped (front must be the right-hand panel), or art mirrored
- Lettering painted into the art on the spine under 79 pages (preflight only sees live text)
- Back cover blurb missing, cut off, misspelled or over busy art without a soft panel
- Barcode area not clear: anything other than flat background in the bottom-right ~2 x 1.2 in of the back cover
- Title, author or a character's face crossing the spine folds or within ~0.375 in of the trim
- Text hard to read at thumbnail size on the front (title must read at ~1 in wide)
- Visible seam, colour jump or gap where back, spine and front meet
Minor: blurb line breaks, small spacing differences from the style guide.
