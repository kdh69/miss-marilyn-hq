# QC rubric — art_page

Compare against the brief, the page plan text for this page and the character sheet.
Resolution and bleed are checked by preflight, not by you.

Blocking:
- Any text, letters or watermark in the image
- Character off-model: markings, colors, proportions or number of limbs/paws/tails wrong
- Scene doesn't match the page plan; important content near the edge (outer ~4% of the image)
- Anatomy errors a child would notice (extra fingers/paws, merged bodies)
Minor: palette drift, background clutter.
