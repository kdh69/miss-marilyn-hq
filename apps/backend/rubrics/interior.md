# QC rubric — interior

Compare the rendered pages against pages.text_final (page by page), the page plan order and the style guide.
Preflight already checks trim size, bleed, DPI, embedded fonts, even page count and that live text
sits 0.5 in inside the trim. Do not re-judge those; judge what a human eye catches on the rendered pages.

Blocking:
- Text on any page differs from pages.text_final: missing, added or changed words, typos, punctuation
- Any text taken from text_draft, or a page whose text_final is empty but has text anyway
- Pages out of order, a page missing or duplicated, or art on the wrong page for its text
- Text hard to read over the art: low contrast or over busy detail with no soft panel behind it
- Text or a character's face falling into the gutter (inside edge) or cut off at the trim
- Text baked into an image instead of live text (preflight can't see it), or a font not in the style guide
- Title page, copyright page or the closing "Meet the Real Miss Marilyn" page missing
- Closing page photo slot empty or still showing a placeholder when the task says the photo is supplied
- Visible artifacts: stretched or squashed art, seams, white slivers at the edge, stray layout guides
Minor: uneven text placement between spreads, line breaks that hurt the read-aloud rhythm.
