# QC rubric — marketing_plan

Compare against the products row, the brief, the series bible, the plans rows this task wrote
(plans.task_id) and Scout's research rows cited. One plan per book; the Marketer's weekly
social_batch tasks follow it, and the owner posts by hand, so the plan must fit her time.

Blocking:
- Missing sections: at least one goal, one audience, 3-5 pillars, one platform row for each of
  Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and TikTok, launch rows (before, launch week, after), one hashtags
  row per platform, and at least two kpi rows
- A platform row without its pace (posts per week), best days or times, formats (pin, reel,
  carousel, post, story, short) and what each post links to
- Total posting load over about 15 minutes a day for the owner, launch week excepted
- Hype or claims we can't back ("bestselling", "teaches reading", awards), or speaking to children
  instead of parents and gift-givers
- Anything that needs photos of children, real customers' names, paid ads or spending money
- Hashtags that name another brand, book, character or author, or more than 15 on one platform
- Rows missing product_id, task_id, section or sort, or sections out of the plan_section list
Minor: pillars that don't lead with the real cat; research rows not cited; vague kpis.
