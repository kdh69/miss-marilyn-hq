# QC rubric — printable

Compare against the brief (what the printable is, page count, which product it belongs to) and the style guide.
Preflight already checks page size (US Letter and A4 versions), DPI and embedded fonts. Do not re-judge those.

Blocking:
- Only one of the two versions delivered, or the two versions differ in content
- Anything important cut off or within ~0.5 in of the page edge (home printers can't print to the edge)
- Text misspelled, wrong, or not matching the brief; any text from a book that isn't in pages.text_final
- Coloring pages: outlines broken, too thin to colour (under ~1.5 pt), or grey fills instead of clean line art
- Activity doesn't work: maze with no path, matching pairs that don't match, counting answers wrong
- Character off-model compared with the character sheet
- Heavy full-page dark backgrounds that waste a parent's ink without a reason in the brief
- Not usable by ages 2–6: tiny detail, fiddly cutting, or reading required with no adult prompt
Minor: layout balance, a tighter crop on the art, page order within the set.
